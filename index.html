Project · Proposal

How I'd structure governance for this workstream.

The problem

Delivering a two-country data-migration and system-integration workstream into a new Group ERP platform within a fixed 18-month window, while governing one or more external technical integrators. The main exposure is not the ERP configuration itself — that sits with the implementation partner — but delivery risk on the workstream around it: data integrity loss during migration, schedule slippage from uncoordinated cross-country dependencies, and quality escapes from insufficient testing discipline ahead of cutover.

The proposed solution

A standardized, previously proven data-migration and integration-governance methodology, not dependent on prior familiarity with the specific ERP platform: structured feeder/data validation design, functional testing and UAT gating before every promotion, and a vendor/integrator governance model that keeps external technical teams accountable to scope, data quality, and schedule. Platform-specific (D365 F&O) fluency is built in parallel, in direct coordination with the implementation partner's functional and technical leads, without that ramp-up sitting on the program's critical path.
