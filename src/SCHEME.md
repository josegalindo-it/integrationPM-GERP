# Governing data-migration and integration risk on a two-country Group ERP rollout.

> A proposal for the Group ERP / D365 F&O Integration PM role: delivery discipline and governance rigor, not dependent on prior hands-on D365 experience.

## Motivation

### This is the kind of engagement I actively look for.

This program sits squarely in the type of engagement I actively look for: a fixed-scope, time-boxed data-migration and integration workstream inside a larger Group ERP rollout, spanning two countries and requiring close governance of external technical integrators. That specific combination — multi-country, multi-vendor, deadline-driven, embedded in a larger regulated corporate program — is where delivery discipline and governance rigor matter as much as, or more than, prior hands-on familiarity with the specific ERP platform.

## What I bring

### Twenty years managing exactly this kind of delivery risk.

Over 20 years directing complex, multi-technology integration and data-migration programs for Tier-1 banking, insurance, and telecommunications groups. I am not a Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations specialist, and I want to be upfront about that. What I bring instead is a proven system for managing exactly the risk this workstream carries:

- **Data migration governance:** directed the technical delivery of a Credit Risk Data Warehouse migration component (~11,000 hours), cutting the time needed to onboard data into the target platform by roughly 20% through a standardized feeder-migration and validation methodology across legacy (COBOL/CICS/DB2, AS/400) and target Data Warehouse environments.
- **Integration quality under multi-technology conditions:** on a three-stream integration program (mainframe Host, Java front-end, SOA/Web Services), raised pre-production defect detection above 85% through structured functional testing and UAT, protecting production from integration failures.
- **Multi-vendor / integrator governance:** directed a hybrid delivery ecosystem of 6 external technical factories on an 82,000-hour/year program, sustaining ~92% plan compliance.
- **Cross-country program coordination:** aligned a 17-project, ~150,000-hour international portfolio between corporate and regional teams, recovering 4–6 weeks of accumulated schedule delay through structured dependency management and replanning.

PMP® and ITIL® 4 Foundation certified.

## Project · Proposal {nav="Project"}

### How I'd structure governance for this workstream.

#### The problem

Delivering a two-country data-migration and system-integration workstream into a new Group ERP platform within a fixed 18-month window, while governing one or more external technical integrators. The main exposure is not the ERP configuration itself — that sits with the implementation partner — but delivery risk on the workstream around it: data integrity loss during migration, schedule slippage from uncoordinated cross-country dependencies, and quality escapes from insufficient testing discipline ahead of cutover.

#### The proposed solution

A standardized, previously proven data-migration and integration-governance methodology, not dependent on prior familiarity with the specific ERP platform: structured feeder/data validation design, functional testing and UAT gating before every promotion, and a vendor/integrator governance model that keeps external technical teams accountable to scope, data quality, and schedule. Platform-specific (D365 F&O) fluency is built in parallel, in direct coordination with the implementation partner's functional and technical leads, without that ramp-up sitting on the program's critical path.

#### Conceptual architecture

*engagement governance — not D365 technical/solution architecture, which remains the implementation partner's scope*

- **Workstreams:** Data Migration & Quality · System Integration (legacy-to-D365 interfaces) · Testing & Cutover (SIT/UAT/Go-Live) · Vendor/Integrator Governance · Cross-Country Risk & Dependency Management (Spain/Portugal).
- **Cadence:** weekly workstream sync; bi-weekly steering update; monthly cross-country stakeholder review.
- **Gates:** migration dry-runs and mock cutovers ahead of go-live, with defect-detection thresholds before any promotion to production — the same discipline applied on prior multi-technology integration programs.
- **Boundary, stated explicitly:** this architecture governs delivery, risk, and vendor accountability. It does not extend into the D365 F&O technical/solution architecture itself, which is outside this proposal's scope and outside verifiable prior experience.
